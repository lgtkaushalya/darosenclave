Notes from Kamburugamuwa

Our family home sits three kilometres from Mirissa, in a village that most visitors pass through on the way somewhere else. We write about what we know: the research being done along this coast, the place itself, and how to spend a long stretch of time here well. Whale watching and the beaches are here too. They just aren't the point.
