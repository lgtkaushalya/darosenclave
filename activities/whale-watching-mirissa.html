Whale-Watching Mornings from the Villa

A short ride to Mirissa Harbour, then back to a quiet house.

From the Villa to the Harbour

← Back

Whale-watching boats leave from Mirissa Harbour, about 10 minutes by car from Daro's Enclave. This page covers how a morning on the water fits around a stay at the house.

For everything else, read Whale Watching in Mirissa: A Complete Guide to Blue Whales. It covers ethical operators, what to expect at sea, and practical tips.

Calm-Sea Months

Boats run from November to April, when the sea on this coast is calm, with the peak from December to March. Tours are usually suspended during the southwest monsoon, from May to September. The guide has the season in more detail.

The Morning

  • Boats typically leave between 6:00 and 7:00 a.m.
  • Leave the house around 5:45 a.m. The harbour is about 10 minutes away, along quiet coastal roads before sunrise.
  • Most trips are back by early afternoon, leaving the rest of the day for rest, reading or work at the house.

Explore Nearby

  • Sea Bath at Nearby Beaches: Refresh with a swim after your boat trip
  • Surfing at Weligama: Just 15 minutes away, a great follow-up adventure
  • Local Photo Spots: Capture Mirissa's iconic palm tree hill
